Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 10, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how AutomationCalculators.net collects, uses, and protects information when you use the website, create an account, subscribe to communications, purchase Colotti Pulse, or interact with related services.

Depending on how you use the website and services, we may collect information such as:

Purchases of Colotti Pulse and other paid digital products may be processed through third-party payment providers such as Paddle.

Payment providers may collect and process payment information, billing information, tax information, and other transaction data required to complete your purchase.

When Colotti Pulse is purchased or activated, information may be processed as necessary to create, deliver, validate, and manage software licenses.

This information is used to provide the software license purchased by the customer, prevent unauthorized use, and provide support.
