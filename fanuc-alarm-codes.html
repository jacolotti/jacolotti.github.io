Featured Field Fix: SRVO-018 Brake Abnormal

Colotti Automation Field Verified: A FANUC robot showed SRVO-018 Brake Abnormal on Group 1 Axis 2. The fault occurred while trying to move the robot, even when it was not welding. The root cause was tied to a broken, wet, dirty, or contaminated weld gun power cable connection.

This is the kind of fault that can send a team down the wrong path replacing brake hardware, servo amplifiers, or robot harnesses. On welding robots, inspect the weld gun power cable connections and dress package before assuming the brake circuit is the only possible cause.

Basic FANUC Robot Fault Troubleshooting Flow

  1. Record the exact alarm code, group, axis, and full pendant message.
  2. Identify whether the fault happens while jogging, during automatic motion, during welding, during homing, or at power-up.
  3. Check what changed recently: cables, tooling, servo amp, batteries, weld gun, dress package, safety device, or program change.
  4. Inspect connectors, cables, moisture, contamination, loose terminals, and grounding before replacing major components.
  5. Use OEM procedures for electrical checks, mastering, battery replacement, and servo troubleshooting.
  6. Document the final root cause so the same downtime does not happen again.
Safety reminder: Robot troubleshooting can involve stored energy, servo power, brake circuits, weld power, pinch points, and hazardous motion. Follow lockout, safety, and OEM procedures.
