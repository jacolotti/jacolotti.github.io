Recommended Troubleshooting Workflow

1. Record the exact alarm code and full alarm text.
2. Record the group and axis.
3. Determine when the fault occurs: power-up, jogging, automatic cycle, welding, or random.
4. Determine what changed recently.
5. Check cables, connectors, and dress package before replacing major components.
6. Follow OEM procedures for electrical testing and verification.
