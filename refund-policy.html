Refund Policy

Last updated: August 10, 2026

This Refund Policy applies to paid digital products and software purchased through AutomationCalculators.net, including Colotti Pulse.

Summary: Refund requests may be considered within 14 days of purchase. Because Colotti Pulse is downloadable software delivered with a software license key, refunds are reviewed based on the circumstances of the purchase, activation status, and whether the product is functioning as described.

1. Refund Request Window

Refund requests should be submitted within 14 days of the original purchase date.

Requests submitted after 14 days may be considered at our discretion, but are not guaranteed.

2. Colotti Pulse Software Purchases

Colotti Pulse is a digital software product delivered electronically with one or more software license keys.

A refund may be considered when:

  • The software cannot be activated due to a verified licensing issue.
  • The software does not function as materially described for a supported use case.
  • A duplicate purchase was made accidentally.
  • An incorrect quantity of seats was purchased and the unused seats can be identified.
  • A technical issue cannot be reasonably resolved through support.

3. Activated Licenses

Because each Colotti Pulse license is intended for activation on a specific computer, activation status may be considered when reviewing a refund request.

A fully activated and successfully functioning license may not qualify for a refund simply because the customer later decides not to use the software.

This does not limit any rights that may apply under applicable law.

4. Multi-Seat Purchases

When multiple seats are purchased, each license key represents a separate software activation.

If only part of a multi-seat purchase is affected, we may consider a partial refund for unused or unresolved seats where appropriate.

5. Change of Mind

Because Colotti Pulse is a digital product and license keys are delivered immediately after purchase, refunds based solely on a change of mind are not guaranteed once the software has been successfully activated and used.

If you are unsure whether Colotti Pulse is appropriate for your application, contact us before purchasing.

6. Compatibility

Customers are responsible for confirming that their intended PLC, computer, network, and software environment are suitable for Colotti Pulse.

If you have compatibility questions before purchasing, contact us and we will provide reasonable guidance based on the information available.

7. Technical Support Before Refund

For technical issues, we may ask for reasonable troubleshooting information before approving a refund.

This may include details about the computer, software version, activation status, PLC model, network conditions, or error messages.

The goal is to determine whether the issue can be resolved before canceling the purchase.

8. Payment Processor

Payments may be processed by Paddle or another authorized payment provider.

Approved refunds may be issued through the original payment method and may be subject to the payment processor's processing times and policies.

9. Fraud, Abuse, and License Misuse

Refunds may be denied in cases involving suspected fraud, chargeback abuse, unauthorized redistribution, license sharing, attempts to bypass activation controls, or other misuse of the software or licensing system.
